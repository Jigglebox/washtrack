WashTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

WashTrack (the "App") is operated by ES&D Services Inc. ("we," "us," or "our"). The App is a workforce and client tool for tracking vehicle-wash work, billing, and related operations. This policy explains what information the App collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Who the App is for

WashTrack is intended for two groups of authorized users: (1) our field and office employees, and (2) approved client-portal users at facilities we service. Access is granted by account and role; the App is not intended for the general public and is not directed to children.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password (passwords are stored securely by our authentication provider; we never see them in plain text). For employees, we also store an employee ID, role, and assigned locations.
  • Work and billing data — records you create or that relate to you, such as logged wash entries, work items, vehicles, service locations, and related notes and messages.
  • Location information — if you grant permission, the App may use your device's location while you are using it to help confirm you are at the correct job site when logging work. Location is used only in the foreground, only for this purpose, and you can decline or revoke the permission in your device settings at any time.
  • Client-portal information — for portal users, the facilities you are associated with, wash history for those facilities, and wash requests you submit.
  • Basic technical/diagnostic data — limited information needed to keep you signed in and to diagnose errors you choose to report.

How we use your information

We use the information above only to operate and support the service: to authenticate you, to record and bill for work, to show you your relevant history and messages, to verify job-site presence when you log work, to respond to issues you report, and to maintain the security and integrity of the system.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. Information is visible within your organization according to your role (for example, office staff can see employee work records; portal users see only their own facilities). We share data with service providers only as needed to run the App — principally our backend and authentication provider, which stores the data on our behalf. We may disclose information if required by law.

Data storage and security

Your data is stored on managed cloud infrastructure and protected by access controls and row-level security so that each user can access only the data their role permits. While no system is perfectly secure, we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Data retention

We retain account and work data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service and meet legal, accounting, and business requirements. You may request deletion of your account by contacting us (see below); some records may be retained where required for legitimate business or legal reasons.

Your choices and rights

  • You can decline or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at the address below.
  • You can stop using the App and ask us to deactivate your account.

Children's privacy

The App is for authorized adult employees and client users and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, communicated in the App.
